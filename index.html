Who We Are

Building the
Future of Infrastructure

Headquartered in Ughelli, Delta State, with branch offices in Abuja and Lagos and an equipment base in Warri, we deliver civil construction, fabrication, procurement, and logistics services directly and in collaboration with technical partners.

What We Do

Comprehensive Engineering Solutions

Focused engineering services delivered directly and with technical partners, across the full asset and project life cycle.

Civil Construction

Infrastructure development; buildings, roads, earthworks, and steel structures.

Fabrication & Machining

Machining and fabrication of precise parts and components under strict quality control.

General Logistics

Hire of heavy-duty earth-moving equipment and supply of construction materials.

Mechanical Procurement

Sourcing of mechanical equipment and spares, from pumps and gearboxes to seals, bearings, couplings and wire rope for heavy and light industrial needs.

Instrument & Electrical
